# Hero background image / video

The hero section can show a full-bleed background image or video. Configure it in **content/pageData.json**:

- **heroImage** – path to an image, e.g. `/hero.jpg` (file in `public/`)
- **heroVideo** – path to a video, e.g. `/hero.mp4` (file in `public/`). If both are set, video is used.

Leave both empty to keep the default gradient background.

## Where to put files

- Put images and videos in **public/** (e.g. `public/hero.jpg`, `public/hero.mp4`).
- In pageData use the public URL: `"/hero.jpg"` or `"/hero.mp4"`.

## Choosing something that fits (and isn’t cliché)

**Avoid:** Generic “team high-fiving”, obvious stock “business handshake”, glowing laptops on desks, and overloaded “tech” or “startup” imagery. They read as filler.

**Better directions:**

- **Calm and local:** Stockholm cityscape (water, rooftops, soft light), Swedish nature or workshop/atelier that feels small-scale and real. Suggests “we’re here, we’re grounded” without saying “corporate”.
- **Craft and clarity:** Simple workspace (desk, tools, paper, one device), clean interior, or abstract shapes/textures in your brand tones (e.g. warm neutrals + accent). Suggests “we care about craft and clarity”.
- **Motion (video):** Gentle, loopable clips: water, leaves, traffic from a distance, or a slow pan over a quiet scene. Keep it slow and mute-friendly; the overlay keeps text readable.

**Where to look (free/royalty-free):**

- **Pexels** (pexels.com) – search e.g. “Stockholm aerial”, “minimal office”, “craft workspace”, “abstract warm gradient”, “slow motion water”.
- **Coverr** (coverr.co) – short, loop-friendly stock videos; filter by “business” or “nature” and pick calm, non-dramatic clips.
- **Unsplash** (unsplash.com) – “Stockholm”, “Scandinavian workspace”, “minimal desk”, “warm texture”.

**Technical tips:**

- **Image:** 1920×1080 or 1920×1200 is enough; compress (e.g. 80–85% quality) so the file stays small.
- **Video:** 10–15 s loop, 1080p, ~1–2 Mbps, muted. Prefer MP4 (H.264) for compatibility; optional WebM for smaller size.
